# Office Hours: Questions and Support

    - Look for project group (ex. allstate-1, accenture-1-a)

Office hours are not just regular office hours they are a **virtual co-working space** that uses **body doubling** to be productive. Come with work, any work, and focus with other peeps.

## What is Body Doubling

Body doubling is a productivity and focus strategy where you complete a task in the presence of another person, virtually or in person. Your companion does not need to help you or do the same work; their quiet, shared presence provides gentle accountability and helps reduce procrastination.

**How this work in Office Hours**

- *Shared Presence:* Camera on, mics off. Cameras off for breaks or if you are afk (away from keyboard). This can be in the main room or in shared breakout rooms.
- *Independent Tasks:* You work on your goal, does not have to be BTT related or even course related. Need to remove the procrastination from washing dishes or folding laundry? Come in, camera on, mic off and get it done.
- *Need help?:* This is still office hours at the end of the day. Ask for help - BTT work is the priority to get help but your coach is here to help. To keep the main room a focus room follow [these steps](#how-to-ask-for-help) to keep that room a focus room.

## Using the Office Hours space

| Space | How to use it |
| --- | --- |
| **Main Room** | Focus room for **Body Doubling**. Come in camera on, mic off, and get whatever your goal is done. |
| **Breakout Rooms** | Use these for team work, small-group problem-solving, or quiet work. Request that your coach join though tagging them on Discord. |
| **Coach Office** | This is a private space for one-on-one conversations. Request a private conversation through Discord or by using the Zoom chat. |

## How to ask for help

1. Join the Zoom room. Find this in Discord 
    - Under your section (ex. Fall AI Studio A)
    - Look for project group (ex. allstate-1, accenture-1-a)
    - Look for the office hours post where your teams posts are
2. If your question is small ask it! If no one if there come on mic and let's solve whatever is going on. If peeps are focus working, @ me in the Office Hours Post and let's move to a breakout room.
    - Normally there is not a queue in office hours but if there is keeping messages in the Discord Office Hours post will naturally create a queue so no one is missed, skipped, or left behind.

## Working agreements

- Come ready to work, collaborate, or ask a focused question.
- Keep ownership of your work: explain your thinking and try an approach before asking your coach to solve it.
- Use Breakout Rooms respectfully and leave room for other teams to work.
- Keep the public queue concise. Do not share private details there; message your coach when a private conversation is needed.
- If your question is resolved before your coach arrives, update your queue post to say so.

## What your coach will do

- Acknowledge requests and work through them as fairly and quickly as possible.
- Help you clarify the problem, consider options, and identify a next step without taking ownership of your work.
- Let you know when they are helping another Fellow or team, so you understand the queue.
- Make space for private conversations when needed and treat sensitive concerns respectfully.
- Help create a supportive, focused space where everyone can ask questions, learn, and make progress.
- If a problem cannot be fully resolved during Office Hours, leave with a clear next step and any needed follow-up.
