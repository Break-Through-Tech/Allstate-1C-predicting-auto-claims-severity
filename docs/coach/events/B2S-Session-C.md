### Challenge Advisor Intro Meeting

In this section you will be meeting with your challenge advisor. This is the time to connect with them and establish a working relationship with them.

> Challenge Advisors will have two meetings with two teams. This 60 mins block will be broken into two sections each 30 mins. If you do not see your CA during the first half you will see them in the second half. If you are waiting you should be reviewing the project and making a list of questions.

*Tips for this section:*

- Introduce yourselves and have the CA (challenge advisor) introduce themselves.
- Collect the CAs email address and confirm if email or Discord will be the best way to communicate with them.

*Project question tips:*

- What problem or opportunity is most important for our team to understand?
- What would a successful project outcome look like?
- What assumptions about the challenge should we test early?
- What should our team avoid focusing on?
- What questions do you have for us about our team or initial approach?

> Do not feel pressured to know the project inside and out to have a long list of questions for the CA. The goal of this meeting is to meet the CA, you can and should email questions to the CA after this meeting and whenever needed.
